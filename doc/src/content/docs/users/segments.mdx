---
title: Segments
description: Build contact segments to target groups for drip campaign enrollment.
---

Segments are saved contact filters. They let you define a group of contacts by shared attributes — tags, status, company, industry, or LinkedIn presence — and then bulk-enroll that group into a drip campaign with a single click.

## Navigating to Segments

Click **Segments** in the left sidebar.

## Creating a Segment

Click **Create**. Give the segment a name and optional description, then use the **Filter Builder** to define which contacts belong to it.

### Filter Builder

Filters combine with AND logic — a contact must match every criterion you set.

| Filter | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Match contacts by their CRM status (e.g. In contract, Prospect, Lost) |
| **Tags** | Match contacts that have any of the selected tags |
| **Companies** | Match contacts at one or more specific companies (searchable multi-select) |
| **Industry sectors** | Match contacts whose company is in a selected sector |
| **Has LinkedIn** | Match only contacts with a LinkedIn URL on file |

You can combine multiple filters. For example: Status = Prospect + Tags includes "warm-lead" + Industry = SaaS.

### Live Contact Count

Below the filter builder, a live count shows how many contacts currently match your filters. This updates as you adjust the criteria — useful for sanity-checking before enrolling.

## Viewing a Segment

Click any segment in the list to open its detail page. The detail page shows:

- A **Filter Summary** resolving company names, sector labels, and tag names in plain language
- The **live contact count** for the current filter criteria
- An **Enroll** button to enroll matching contacts into a campaign

## Enrolling a Segment

On the segment detail page, click **Enroll**. A dialog appears to select the target campaign. Only **Active** campaigns are available for enrollment.

Click **Enroll** to confirm. The system enrolls every contact currently matching the segment's filters, skipping any contact already enrolled in that campaign. This makes re-running an enrollment safe — it will only pick up new contacts.

:::note
Enrollment is a snapshot at the time you click Enroll. Contacts added later who match the filters are not automatically enrolled — re-run the enrollment to pick them up.
:::

## Editing a Segment

Click **Edit** on the segment detail page to adjust the filters or rename the segment. Changes to filters do not retroactively affect existing enrollments — they only affect future enrollment runs.

## Segment Strategy Tips

- **Use tags for intent signals** — tag contacts as "warm-lead", "event-attendee", or "inbound" to create precise, manageable segments.
- **Sector targeting** — use the industry filter to send sector-specific messaging without creating separate contact lists.
- **Combine company + status** — narrow to contacts at target accounts who are not yet in contract.
- **Check count before enrolling** — the live count preview prevents accidentally enrolling thousands of contacts into the wrong campaign.
