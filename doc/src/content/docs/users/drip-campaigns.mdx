---
title: Drip Campaigns
description: Create and manage multi-step email drip campaigns in epiSolve CRM.
---

Drip campaigns let you send a sequence of automated emails to contacts over time. Each campaign is made up of steps — individual emails sent at configurable intervals. Contacts are enrolled via segments, and the system tracks every delivery, open, click, bounce, and unsubscribe.

## Navigating to Campaigns

Click **Campaigns** in the left sidebar. The campaign list shows all campaigns with their status badge (Draft, Active, Paused, Archived).

## Creating a Campaign

Click **Create** in the top right.

- **Name** — the internal label for this campaign
- **Slug** — a URL-safe identifier (auto-derived from the name, editable)
- **Description** — optional context for your team
- **Status** — start with **Draft** while building steps; switch to **Active** when ready to send

Save to open the campaign detail page.

## Managing Steps

Each step is one email in the sequence. Open the **Steps** tab on the campaign detail page.

### Editing a Step

Click any step row to expand it, then click the **pencil icon** to enter edit mode.

- **Subject** — the email subject line. Use `{first_name}` to personalise.
- **Delay (days)** — how many days after the previous step this email sends. Set to `0` for the first step to send on the day of enrollment.
- **Body** — a rich-text editor (bold, italic, links, lists). Use `{first_name}` anywhere in the body too.

Click **Save** to commit changes, or **Cancel** to discard.

### Adding a Step

Click **Add step** below the step list. A new step is appended with a 7-day delay and blank subject/body. Edit it immediately.

:::tip[Step count guidance]
Click the **ⓘ** icon next to Add step to see best-practice guidance on campaign length and spacing. The sweet spot for B2B outreach is **4–6 steps**. A warning appears at 6+ steps; adding is disabled at 8.
:::

### Deleting a Step

Only the **last step** can be deleted (to avoid gaps in active enrollments). A trash icon appears on the final step when there is more than one step. Click it and confirm the inline prompt.

### Sending a Test Email

While editing a step — or with a step expanded in read-only view — a **Send test email** panel appears at the bottom. Enter (or edit) a recipient address and click **Send test**. The address is remembered for future test sends. The test email arrives with a `[TEST]` prefix in the subject and a yellow banner so it's clearly identifiable.

## Analytics

The campaign detail page shows a header with six live metrics:

| Tile | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Enrolled | Total contacts enrolled in this campaign |
| Delivered | Emails confirmed delivered by Resend |
| Open rate | Unique opens ÷ delivered |
| Click rate | Unique clicks ÷ delivered |
| Completed | Contacts who finished all steps |
| Suppressed | Contacts removed due to bounce, complaint, or unsubscribe |

These update in real time as email events arrive from Resend.

## Enrollments Tab

The **Enrollments** tab lists every enrolled contact with their current status:

- **Active (step N)** — in the campaign, waiting for step N to send
- **Completed** — finished all steps
- **Suppressed** — removed (with reason shown on hover)

Each row also shows a mini delivery summary: number of emails sent (✉), opened (👁), and clicked (🖱) for that contact.

## Cloning a Campaign

To duplicate an existing campaign (e.g. to reuse copy for a different audience), click **Clone** in the campaign header. A dialog pre-fills the name as "Copy of [original name]" with a derived slug. Edit either field, then click **Clone**. The new campaign is created as Draft with all steps copied, and you are redirected to it immediately.

## Campaign Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Being built — drip sender skips this campaign |
| Active | Live — drip sender processes enrollments daily |
| Paused | Temporarily stopped — no new sends until Active again |
| Archived | Retired — kept for reference, not processed |

Change status via the **Edit** button on the campaign detail page.
